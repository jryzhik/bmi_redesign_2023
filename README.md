### IMAGE OFFSET (header image focus section)
``` Keyword values:

center: Positions the element at the center of the container both horizontally and vertically.
top: Positions the element at the top center of the container horizontally and at the top vertically.
bottom: Positions the element at the bottom center of the container horizontally and at the bottom vertically.
left: Positions the element at the left center of the container horizontally and at the center vertically.
right: Positions the element at the right center of the container horizontally and at the center vertically.
Length or percentage values:

You can also use length or percentage values to specify the position. For example:
10px 20%: Positions the element 10 pixels from the top and 20% from the left.
50% 50%: Positions the element at the center of the container both horizontally and vertically.
0 100%: Positions the element at the top right corner of the container.
